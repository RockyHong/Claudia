# BUG-004 — the stale-hook banner is flat where its neighbours are glass

**Logged:** 2026-09-10 · **Source:** user review of the banner shipped in `320b1cb`
**Problem:** `HookUpdatePrompt.svelte` paints itself with a flat tint — `background: rgba(229, 160, 58, 0.12)` plus a matching border — and declares no `backdrop-filter` at all. Every surface it sits among is glass: `SessionList` blurs at 20px, `StatusBar` at 16px, the modal layer at 4px, and `App.svelte`'s immersive rules at 24px. It also carries no immersive-mode treatment, so with the avatar video as the page background the banner reads as an opaque slab in a column of translucent ones. It does not match the design language it renders inside.
**Area:** `packages/web/src/lib/HookUpdatePrompt.svelte` (`<style>` block), `design-system.html`, `packages/web/src/App.svelte` (immersive-mode overrides at :538, :598, :687), sibling references `packages/web/src/lib/SessionList.svelte:66` and `packages/web/src/lib/StatusBar.svelte:44`
**Prior:** the component was written without a live render pass — `packages/web` ships no test surface, so the styling was never seen against the real board or in immersive mode. Whatever the fix, it should be checked in-app in both normal and immersive modes rather than by reading CSS.
